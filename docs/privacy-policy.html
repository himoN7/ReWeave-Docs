Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026. ReWeave is a Windows desktop app from ReZephyr. This page describes the public product. It is not a technical guide.

  • You do not need an account to use the design tools.
  • Projects and settings stay on your PC. ReZephyr does not sell personal information and does not run an advertising profile.
  • If you choose a cloud assistant and enter your own key, that conversation is sent to the provider you selected, under that provider’s policy. You can leave that feature off.
  • Links you open, such as documentation or a support message, use your browser or mail app.
  • ReWeave is not directed at children under 13.
